Explainable Solana checks beside the coins on trade.padre.gg.

When enabled, token and market addresses are sent to RugCheck and DEX Screener. Solana’s public RPC verifies large holder accounts and resolves supported pool addresses. The on-chain scan sends public holder addresses and transaction signatures to that RPC. No wallet connection, private keys, account cookies or trading permissions.

For deeper checks

Open a coin → Holders → All → scroll to the top, then visit Trades. Click its risk icon to inspect the score and review missing evidence.

Scores measure observed warning signals, not the probability of a rug. Unknown data never means safe. This version supports Terminal in English.

Local scan history

Up to 500 timestamped evidence snapshots, retained for 7 days. Stored on this browser for later review; no trades or outcomes are inferred.
